Electro-Mechanical Engineering Intern · D&D Motor Systems · Sep 2025 to Jan 2026

Prototype AC/DC Motor Dynamometer

Type
Electromechanical test equipment development
Tools
LabVIEW, National Instruments data acquisition (NI DAQ), SolidWorks Electrical, PID control
Scope
AC and DC motor testing at high power and high speed

Overview

D&D Motor Systems was expanding into the AC motor market, and its existing production dynamometer was becoming unreliable. Commercial production dynamometers can cost upwards of $500,000, so the goal was an in-house system that could give accurate, repeatable performance testing for both AC and DC motors at a fraction of the cost.

The system combined mechanical, electrical and software parts to provide real-time telemetry, motor control and performance monitoring. A key challenge was keeping sensor readings accurate at high speed, where electrical noise can throw off torque and efficiency calculations.

What I Did

  • Worked with the lead engineer on electrical wiring, mechanical assembly and sensor integration for the dynamometer.
  • Integrated telemetry and safety systems, including wiring, sensor installation and troubleshooting.
  • Diagnosed signal quality problems and made filter and wiring changes so the system collected reliable data during high-speed testing.
  • Helped test and validate the company's first AC motor platform on the new dynamometer.
  • Contributed to engineering documentation, testing procedures and data analysis throughout the project.

Results

  • Helped build and commission a prototype production dynamometer that tests both AC and DC motors.
  • Improved measurement reliability through filter adjustments and data acquisition refinements.
  • Compiled and analyzed years of historical motor test data to track long-term efficiency across a motor family, and built a motor database to catch future performance drops.
  • Found a brush manufacturing defect that reduced rotor contact quality, lowering efficiency and causing early motor failures.
  • Traced the defect back to a supply-chain quality problem that had gone undetected.

Endorsed by Ramin Safarpour, Lead Engineer, D&D Motor Systems.
