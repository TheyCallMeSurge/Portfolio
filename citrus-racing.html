Formula SAE Front Wing & Engine Work

Team
Citrus Racing, Syracuse University
Tools
ANSYS Fluent, CAD, 3D printing (TPU)
Areas
Aerodynamics and powertrain

Overview

On the Citrus Racing Formula SAE team I worked on both aerodynamic and powertrain development for the car.

On the aero side I helped develop the initial front wing design for the CR5 car, aimed at generating downforce while balancing weight, durability and ground clearance.

What I Did

  • Ran computational fluid dynamics (CFD) in ANSYS Fluent: built the fluid domain, generated the mesh, and applied moving ground and symmetry boundary conditions with the SST k-ω turbulence model.
  • Post-processed pressure, velocity and streamline results to understand airflow around the wing.
  • Took the wing from CAD to 3D-printed TPU prototypes on Bambu printers for wind tunnel testing.
  • Took part in full engine teardowns and rebuilds, inspecting parts for wear and checking critical dimensions against spec.

Results

  • The CFD study gave qualitative insight into pressure distribution, flow acceleration and downforce generation.
  • During post-processing I noticed the solution had not fully converged, so I documented the limits and the fixes: mesh refinement, inflation layers and convergence monitoring.
  • The main lesson was how to judge simulation quality before trusting the results.
