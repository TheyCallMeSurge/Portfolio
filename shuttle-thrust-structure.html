Space Shuttle Thrust Structure Design

Type
Structural design and analysis
Tools
MATLAB, hand calculations, engineering drawing
Safety factors
Ultimate ≥ 1.5 × limit, yield ≥ 1.25 × limit
Materials
Ti-5Al-2.5Sn titanium, boron-reinforced titanium, boron epoxy

Overview

The goal was to design an idealized upper thrust structure for the Space Shuttle Orbiter that carries the main engine loads, meets strict safety factors, and weighs as little as possible.

Every relevant failure mode had to be checked: Euler buckling, crippling, cylindrical buckling and deflection under combined loading.

What I Did

  • Built a MATLAB load analysis to compute member loads across multiple load cases.
  • Automated safety factor checks for every truss member across the full load envelope.
  • Chose composite or metallic members to minimize weight while meeting every requirement.

Results

  • All structural members passed both yield and ultimate strength requirements.
  • Achieved significant weight savings by using boron epoxy composite members where they fit.
  • Developed a reusable MATLAB pipeline for safety factor verification.
